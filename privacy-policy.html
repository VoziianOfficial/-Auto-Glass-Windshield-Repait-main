How information is handled across this site.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you visit this website, browse auto glass information or submit an enquiry through the contact form.

1. Overview

Company name operates as an independent auto glass services aggregator. The website provides information relating to windshield repair, windshield replacement and related auto glass topics.

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary for operating the site, responding to enquiries and maintaining basic website functionality.

Important

This website does not require you to provide a telephone number. The contact form is designed around name, email, service selection and message information.

2. Information we may collect

The type of information collected depends on how you use the website.

Information you submit

When you use the enquiry form, you may provide:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The auto glass service you are interested in.
  • Information you choose to include in your message.
  • Confirmation that you have accepted the relevant privacy notice for the form.

Basic technical information

Standard hosting or server infrastructure may process technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate request time, referring page, requested page and IP address. The exact information available can depend on the hosting environment used for the website.

Information you should avoid sending

Please do not submit passwords, banking information, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information through the general contact form.

3. How information may be used

Information submitted or processed through the website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to your auto glass enquiry.
  • Understanding whether your message relates to windshield repair, replacement or another relevant request.
  • Maintaining the functionality and security of the website.
  • Identifying technical problems or misuse of the website.
  • Meeting applicable legal or administrative requirements where necessary.

Information should not be used for purposes that are materially incompatible with the reason it was originally collected without an appropriate basis for doing so.

4. Sharing and service providers

Website information may be processed by service providers that support normal site operation, such as hosting, email delivery or technical infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably required to comply with applicable law, legal process or a valid request from an authorised authority.

We do not state that every third-party auto glass provider referenced or ultimately chosen by a user is operated by Company name . Service credentials, insurance, warranties, pricing and provider-specific privacy practices should be confirmed directly with the relevant provider.

Independent providers

If you leave this website or communicate directly with another service provider, their own privacy terms and data practices may apply.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website may use limited browser storage for basic functionality and user preferences. For example, the cookie notice can remember whether it has already been accepted or dismissed.

Essential storage

Site function

May be used to remember basic interface choices such as whether the cookie notice has already been handled.

Technical data

Infrastructure

Hosting systems may process normal technical request information required to deliver and protect the website.

More information is available in our Cookie Policy .

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid sending unnecessary confidential information through the general enquiry form and should use an appropriate secure communication method if a service provider requests sensitive documentation.

7. Data retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining records where appropriate and meeting applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods can vary depending on the nature of the communication, the relevant service and any legal or administrative obligations that apply.

8. Your choices

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights or choices relating to personal information, which can include requesting access, correction or deletion of information in appropriate circumstances.

You can also choose not to submit the contact form and instead contact us directly by email.

Where a request concerns information held by a separate auto glass provider, the request may need to be directed to that provider rather than this website.

9. Third-party websites

The website may contain links or references to resources operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of independent third-party websites.

Before providing personal information to another website or provider, review the privacy information made available by that party.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its functionality or applicable practices change.

The updated version may be published on this page with a revised update date. Continued use of the site after a change means the current version of the policy will apply to subsequent use of the website.

11. Privacy enquiries

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or information submitted through this website, contact us by email.
