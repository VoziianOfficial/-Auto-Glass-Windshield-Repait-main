How this site uses browser storage.

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies, local storage and similar browser technologies may be used when you visit this auto glass services website.

1. Overview

Company name uses limited website storage to support essential functionality and remember basic interface choices.

The current website is designed to operate without unnecessary advertising or complex tracking functionality.

Current site setup

The cookie confirmation card uses browser storage so the website can remember whether you have already accepted or dismissed the notice.

2. What cookies and browser storage are

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website can store through your browser. Websites can also use related technologies such as local storage or session storage.

These technologies can be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, maintaining a session, protecting a website or understanding how a site is used.

Not every browser-storage mechanism is technically a cookie, but this policy uses the term broadly where it makes the explanation easier to understand.

3. Storage currently used by this website

The website may use the following categories of browser or technical storage.

Preference storage

Essential

Used to remember whether the cookie notice has already been accepted or dismissed.

Technical storage

Infrastructure

Hosting or security infrastructure may use temporary technical mechanisms required to deliver and protect the website.

Session data

When required

A hosting environment may use temporary session information where required for normal technical operation.

Third-party storage

Provider dependent

External services added in the future may operate under their own cookie and privacy practices.

4. Essential preference storage

The website currently uses a local storage value for the cookie confirmation interface.

Storage key Purpose Type Duration
site-cookie-consent Remembers whether the cookie notice was accepted or dismissed so it does not repeatedly obstruct normal browsing. Local storage Until browser storage is cleared or the value is removed.

This preference value does not require you to provide your name, email address or telephone number.

5. Why essential storage is used

Essential or functional browser storage may help the website:

  • Remember interface choices.
  • Avoid repeatedly displaying the same notice.
  • Maintain basic website functionality.
  • Support technical security measures.
  • Deliver pages correctly through the hosting infrastructure.

These functions are intended to support normal use of the website rather than build an advertising profile about you.

6. Analytics and advertising

The current website structure does not intentionally include advertising cookies or behavioural advertising functionality.

If analytics, advertising or additional third-party functionality is introduced in the future, this policy should be updated to reflect the relevant technology and how it is used.

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies, appropriate consent controls should be provided before those technologies are activated.

7. Third-party services

Some technical website resources may be provided by third-party infrastructure, hosting or content-delivery services.

A third-party service may process basic technical request information according to its own infrastructure and policies.

If you follow a link to an independent auto glass provider or another external website, that website may use its own cookies and browser-storage technologies.

External websites

This Cookie Policy applies to this website. An independent provider's website can have a different cookie policy and consent system.

8. How you can control browser storage

Most browsers allow you to review, delete or restrict cookies and other stored website data.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • Delete stored website data.
  • Block cookies from selected websites.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Clear local storage.
  • Use private or incognito browsing modes.
  • Configure browser privacy preferences.

Blocking or deleting essential storage can mean that some website preferences need to be selected again.

9. Cookie confirmation card

When the website does not find a previously saved preference, a compact cookie card may be displayed near the bottom of the screen.

Selecting Accept stores an accepted preference. Selecting Close stores a dismissed preference.

Both choices prevent the compact notice from repeatedly appearing on every page during future visits while that preference remains stored in the browser.

10. Contact form

The website contact form collects information that you actively choose to submit. This is separate from the essential cookie preference described above.

Information submitted through the enquiry form may include:

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Selected service.
  • Your message.

More information about handling submitted information is available in the Privacy Policy .

11. Storage duration

Browser-storage duration depends on the type of storage being used.

The site's cookie-notice preference is stored in local storage and can remain there until you clear the relevant browser data or it is otherwise removed.

Temporary technical or session information used by hosting infrastructure may have different retention periods.

12. Privacy

Cookies and browser storage can sometimes involve processing information that is also relevant to privacy law.

For information about personal data, enquiries, retention and privacy-related choices, please review our Privacy Policy .

13. Changes to this Cookie Policy

This policy may be updated if the website begins using new storage technologies, analytics, third-party integrations or other functionality that changes how cookies or similar technologies are used.

The current version may be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
